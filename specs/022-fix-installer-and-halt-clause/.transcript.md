# Session Transcript: fix-installer-and-halt-clause

## Session Overview
A field trial document (`askfirst-advice.md`) reported two independent gaps:
an installer bug that leaves agent hooks silently unregistered, and a
finding that the HALT directive's "never mention a workaround" clause gets
overridden by agents regardless of hook reinforcement, because it
conflicts with a transparency norm. This stage addressed both.

## Design Decisions Made

### Decision 1: Scope covers both the installer bug and the wording clause
**Chosen:** One stage addressing both issues, kept independently reviewable.
**Rationale:** Both originate from the same source document; bundling
avoids fragmenting related context, while independent reviewability limits
cross-contamination risk.
**Tradeoffs:** Larger single diff than splitting into two stages.
**Proposed by:** mpadge

### Decision 2: Loosen, but do not remove, the workaround-mention prohibition
**Chosen:** A workaround may now be mentioned only as a clearly
subordinate, explicitly-labeled aside, after the upstream question is
surfaced first — never as a selectable, co-equal, or "recommended" menu
option.
**Rationale:** Three prior stages (011, 012, 014) hardened this exact
clause after field reports of agents offering workarounds as menu
options; this stage's own reconciliation test showed reinforcement fixes
injection-distrust but not the mention prohibition, since it conflicts
with a stronger transparency norm. The menu-option pattern those stages
fixed had to remain forbidden even while loosening the mention ban.
**Tradeoffs:** First stage to walk back a repeatedly-hardened directive;
carries reversal risk if agents drift back toward menu-style framing.
**Proposed by:** joint

### Decision 3: Fix both canonical copies of the clause
**Chosen:** Revised wording applied to both
`agent-hooks/askfirst-context.txt` (hook-context reinforcement) and
`agent-content/askfirst-stop-consequence.txt` /
`askfirst-notice-prime.txt` (the hook-independent copy embedded directly
in `askfirst_signal()`'s message text, per stage 014).
**Rationale:** These are two independent mechanisms; fixing only one
would leave the self-sufficient stop signal carrying stale wording.
**Proposed by:** agent

## Tradeoffs Considered
- Allowing a workaround as a genuine selectable option (labeled unvetted)
  vs. keeping a hard anti-menu line with only a subordinate mention: the
  hard line was chosen to preserve the specific fix from stages 011/012.
